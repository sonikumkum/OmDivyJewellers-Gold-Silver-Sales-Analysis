## 📌 Project Overview

**Om Divy Jewellers – Gold & Silver Sales Analysis** is a data analytics and business intelligence project focused on analyzing jewellery sales data.

The project uses **Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI** to prepare, analyze, and visualize sales data and generate meaningful business insights.

The main goal is to understand sales performance, product trends, customer patterns, and category-wise performance to support data-driven decision making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze gold and silver jewellery sales
- Understand overall sales performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns
- Explore sales trends
- Perform data cleaning and transformation
- Generate meaningful business insights
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
